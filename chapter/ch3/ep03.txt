"...Whenever something good happens,
I start believing more good things might follow.

One little thing leads to another,
and suddenly, I see so many possibilities.

I don't want to lose this momentum,
so I keep going.
Sometimes, a little too hard.

But I can't help wondering
how far these little moments might take me.

And who knows?
Maybe the next little dot
is just waiting for me to make it..."
